ZTC Pathway Mapper — End-User Guide

Version: 4.1.0  ·  Date: November 15, 2026  ·  Prepared by: Bradley Franklin  ·  Audience: ZTC committee, deans, curriculum & IR staff

What this tool does

The ZTC Pathway Mapper shows how close each active degree, certificate, and transfer pathway is to being fully Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC). Pathway requirements are built in from the official college catalog for 2023–24 through 2026–27. Upload one file, the ZTC course dataset, click Build dashboard, and explore pathways by academic year and courses by term. All processing stays in your browser; data is not uploaded to a server. Open the app: GitHub Pages · chairmanx.tech.

One data file required

Source What it contains
ZTC course dataset (you upload) The Informer export: per-course, per-term ZTC %, survey completion, and section counts
College catalog (built in) Each pathway's requirements, read from the catalog's Degrees and Certificates pages. Nothing to upload; the upload screen lists the catalog years included.

After the first successful build, your session is saved locally until you choose Load new data.

Quick start — 4 steps

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge (Firefox/Safari for spot checks).
  2. Load the ZTC course dataset CSV — click the card or drag-and-drop the file onto it; wait for Loaded.
  3. Click Build dashboard.
  4. Use the selector in the header: Academic year on Pathway and Dashboard, Term on Courses.

A red error banner usually names a missing column or file read problem — fix the export and try again.

Explore with Pathway and Courses tabs plus the Dashboard pullout

1

Dashboard

  • Open from the graphite Dashboard pullout on the far-left edge
  • Needs attention — pathways below 75% (click to open)
  • Institution overview — average-adoption headline plus four KPI actions; click to drill into Pathway or Courses
  • Compare pathways — stacked distribution, five-pathway ranking, progressive details, and a trend modal
  • Shows the selected academic year, its survey completion, and In progress while a term is incomplete
  • Export: All pathways summary (CSV) only
2

Pathway

  • Select a pathway in the sidebar (search & type filters)
  • Adoption ring and the catalog's sections (e.g. Required Core, List A), with choices shown as "Choose 2 of 6" or "Either … or …"
  • Scored for the selected academic year against that year's catalog
  • Click a course row for detail modal + catalog link (always available in Pathway modal)
  • Export: Current pathway CSV & PDF
3

Courses

  • Search, filter by ZTC status (including CO ZTC), sort by ZTC % / name / survey
  • Detail panel matches the pathway course modal
  • View in course catalog — shown for the two most recent terms only; older terms show an availability note
  • Uses the Term selector; Pathway and Dashboard use Academic year

Course badges

Indicator Meaning
ZTC ≥ 75% of sections zero textbook cost (Chaffey internal bar)
Partial 51–74% of sections ZTC
Not ZTC Below 51%
Purple CO ZTC ≥ one ZTC section (Chancellor's Office) — may appear without green ZTC

Pathway adoption %

Adoption is requirement-based, not a simple course average:

  1. Each catalog requirement counts once: a required course, or each course a choice asks for ("Any two courses" is two requirements).
  2. A requirement is met when a course that can fill it is ZTC (75%+ of sections). Courses taken together (lecture with lab) must all be ZTC.
  3. For an academic year (Fall, Spring, Summer) a course must be at 75%+ in every term it was offered; the course detail names any term that missed.
  4. A pathway is green only at 100%, yellow at 75–99%, red below 75%. Chancellor's Office ZTC is for reporting and never counts toward a pathway.

In progress means the year's surveys are under 90% complete or a term is not in the dataset yet, so the score can still change. Unverified section means the app's reading of that catalog section did not add up to the units the catalog prints; select the “i” beside it for details.

Reliability, security & how to access the app

The mapper is built for long-term committee and IR use. Recent hardening and hosting choices keep the tool stable, auditable, and appropriate for institutional data — without sending your CSV exports to a cloud server.

Topic What it means for you
Your data stays local CSV files are read and processed only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to Chaffey servers or third-party analytics hosts when you build a dashboard.
Recommended access Use the hosted app at chairmanx.tech/projects/ztc-mapper. It always includes the full application (not just a single HTML file).
Pinned, offline-ready libraries Core libraries (React, charts, PDF export, styling) are version-locked and bundled with the app instead of loading from public CDNs that can change without notice. Committee demos work reliably even on restricted networks.
Deploy checks Before each release, automated checks verify that bundled files match an integrity manifest — reducing supply-chain drift and “it worked yesterday” breakage.
Accessibility baseline WCAG 2.1 Level AA target; regression-tested with axe-core across multiple UI states (landing, dashboard, pathway, courses, both themes).
If someone emails you a file Do not open a lone index.html attachment — it will not run. Use the hosted link above, or unzip/copy the entire ztc-mapper folder (including vendor/ and data/) for offline use.

Portfolio site: chairmanx.tech describes the project; the mapper itself lives at the projects link above.

Tips & troubleshooting

  1. 1 Build dashboard disabled — the ZTC course dataset must show Loaded before build unlocks.
  2. 2 A pathway is missing — pathways come from the catalog for the selected academic year; a pathway added or retired in another year appears only in that year.
  3. 3 Course shows “no data” — not in the ZTC course dataset for that term or year, or not offered.
  4. 4 Score looks low for the current year — check for In progress; a year is scored on every term offered so far, and unfinished surveys count as not ZTC.
  5. 5 Stale session — use Load new data and upload a fresh export.
  6. 6 Storage limit banner — browser storage is full; export if needed, then Load new data or clear site data.
  7. 7 Catalog link missing (Courses tab) — the Colleague link appears only for the two most recent terms in your dataset; use Pathway course modal for older terms, or switch term.
  8. 8 Blank page or “missing files” — you opened index.html without the vendor/ and data/ folders; use the hosted app instead.
  9. 9 Accessibility — Tab to skip link; Esc closes modals; keyboard works in nav, term select, and export menu; drag-and-drop announces success via screen reader.
