itself. An answer from the curriculum office or catalog editor closes each one: record
it in `reviewed-sections.json` (see the README), or fix the catalog export if the
catalog itself is corrected.

| Pathway | Section | Years | Catalog prints | Courses allow |

| Computer Information Systems - A.S. Degree | Plus a professional emphasis selection from the options below (minimum | 2023–24, 2024–25, 2025–26 | 12–14 | 24–29 |
| Computer Information Systems - Certificate of Achievement | Plus a professional emphasis selection from the options below (minimum | 2023–24, 2024–25, 2025–26 | 12–14 | 24–29 |

Computer Information Systems (A.S. and certificate): "Plus a professional emphasis selection from the options below (minimum of 12 units)" lists eight emphases of four or five courses each. A student picks one emphasis and takes 12 units from it, which is three or four courses depending on the emphasis. The app has no rule for "one of several lists, each with its own course count", so the section is listed and flagged. Options: score the emphasis closest to ZTC, score every emphasis and show the best, or leave it unscored. The 2026-27 catalog no longer prints this section.
